# Industrial Cooling Water Pump Technical Manual
Document Code: MEC-MAN-CSP-003
Target Equipment: M-301 (Primary Chilled Water Pump)

## 1. System Specifications
- **Flow Rate**: 450 L/min
- **Operating Differential Pressure**: 4.5 - 5.5 bar
- **Normal Liquid Temperature**: 15°C - 35°C
- **Vibration Limit**: < 2.0 mm/s RMS

## 2. Fault Diagnosis & Repair Instructions

### Issue: Pressure Drop & Mechanical Seal Leakage
**Symptoms**: Discharge pressure dropping below 4.0 bar, liquid dripping from pump lantern drain aperture.

**Mechanical Seal Replacement Procedure**:
1. Close suction valve V-01 and discharge valve V-02. Drain pump casing via bottom plug.
2. Remove coupling spacer and unbolt pump back-pullout unit.
3. Unscrew impeller nut (left-hand thread) and remove bronze impeller.
4. Extract damaged mechanical seal faces (silicon carbide / graphite).
5. Clean seal chamber thoroughly with solvent cleaner.
6. Install new mechanical seal kit (Part # MEC-SEAL-PMP-301), ensuring O-rings are lubricated with silicone grease.
7. Re-fit impeller with fresh threadlocker and torque nut to 80 Nm.
8. Re-open valves, purge trapped air from casing vent cock, and verify discharge pressure reaches > 5.0 bar.

## 3. Maintenance Checklist
- **Weekly**: Check gland/seal drip rate (< 5 drops/min normal for packed glands; zero drip for mechanical seals).
- **Monthly**: Measure suction pressure, discharge pressure, and differential head curve.
- **Bi-Annually**: Inspect impeller wear ring clearance (< 0.3 mm limit).
